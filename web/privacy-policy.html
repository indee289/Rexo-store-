Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rexo Collab (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our influencer-marketing platform and mobile application. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

Information we collect

  • Account & profile: name, email, username, profile photo, bio, and (optionally) phone number.
  • Social data: social handles and linked-account details you choose to connect for campaign participation.
  • Financial data: payout details (e.g. UPI/bank) and transaction history needed to process payments and withdrawals.
  • Content: in-app chat messages, campaign applications, and submissions.
  • Device data: device identifiers and push-notification tokens (via Firebase Cloud Messaging and OneSignal).
  • Usage data: how you interact with the app, for functionality, security, and improvement.

The app does not collect your location.

How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account and profile.
  • Match creators with relevant brand campaigns.
  • Process wallet deposits, withdrawals, and campaign payments.
  • Send you service and campaign notifications.
  • Detect and prevent fraud and abuse, and moderate content.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

Sharing and disclosure

We share limited profile information between brands and creators to facilitate collaborations, and with service providers who process data on our behalf (hosting, storage, push notifications, and payment processing). We do not sell your personal information for advertising.

Data storage and security

Data is stored on secure servers (Supabase) and object storage (Cloudflare R2), encrypted in transit. We use role-based access controls, optional two-factor authentication, and session management to protect accounts. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your rights and choices

  • Access, correct, or export your personal data.
  • Delete your account and personal data — in the app under Settings → Delete Account, or via our account deletion page.
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing.

Data retention

When you delete your account, your profile and personal data are deleted or anonymized. Financial and transaction records are retained in anonymized form for the period required by applicable Indian tax and financial regulations, after which they are removed.

Children’s privacy

Rexo Collab is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be indicated by an updated “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, in-app notice.
