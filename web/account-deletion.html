Delete Your Rexo Collab Account

Rexo Collab (com.rexo.marketplace) lets you delete your account and associated personal data at any time. You can do this yourself inside the app, or request deletion by email using the form details below.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Rexo Collab app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, tick the acknowledgement, and tap “Delete my account permanently.”
  4. Your personal data is deleted/anonymized immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Send an email to privacy@rexoagency.in from the email address registered to your account, with the subject “Account Deletion Request.” We verify ownership of the account before processing and complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, username, profile photo, bio, and contact details
  • Your notifications and device/push identifiers
  • Your chat messages (removed from conversations)
  • Your linked social accounts and saved addresses

What is retained (and why)

  • Financial records (wallet, deposits, withdrawals, and transactions) are retained in anonymized form — no longer linked to your personal identity — to meet Indian tax and financial-regulation requirements. See our Privacy Policy for the retention period.
