Delete your Kixo account & data

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

Open Kixo and go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. Confirm on the Delete Account screen. Your account is deleted immediately server-side and you are signed out on all devices.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email (no app needed)

Email us from the address associated with your account and we will verify your identity and process the deletion:

Request account deletion

Send to infokixoads@gmail.com with your registered email/username. We respond within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, photo, bio, public presence).
  • Messages, follows, notifications, linked social accounts.
  • Saved addresses, devices and sessions.
  • Files you uploaded (stored media).
  • Active campaigns you own are cancelled.

What is retained (and for how long)

  • Financial and transaction records (deposits, withdrawals, payments) are retained in anonymized form only for the period required by tax and financial law, then removed.

See our Privacy Policy for full details on data retention.
