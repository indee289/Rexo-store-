Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. This Privacy Policy explains how Kixo may collect, use, disclose, retain and protect information when you access or use Kixo's websites, applications, creator and brand tools, campaign marketplace, communications and related services (collectively, the "Services").

1. Important Vocabulary

In this Privacy Policy, "Kixo," "we," "us," and "our" refer to the Kixo platform and its applicable operating entity. "You" and "your" refer to a visitor, creator, brand, advertiser, customer or other person who uses the Services.

Personal Information means information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked with an individual, where treated as personal information under applicable law.
Creator means a person or organization using the Services to participate in creator or influencer campaigns.
Brand means a business, advertiser or other organization using the Services to create, manage or participate in campaigns.
Campaign means a marketing, advertising, creator collaboration or promotional opportunity made available through the Services.
Services means Kixo's websites, applications, dashboards, campaign marketplace, account features, communications and related products.

2. When Does This Privacy Policy Apply?

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through Kixo's online and mobile Services, including information submitted when you create an account, create or apply to campaigns, communicate with other users, manage a profile, receive or make payments, contact support, or otherwise interact with Kixo.

This Policy does not necessarily apply to information collected by third-party websites, applications, payment providers, social networks or other services that Kixo does not control. Those services may have their own privacy policies.

3. How Do We Collect Personal Information?

We may collect information through the following methods:

3.1 Information You Provide

  • Creating an account: name, email address, phone number, username, password or authentication information and other account details.
  • Creator profiles: profile photo, biography, category or niche, social handles, audience information, location and campaign-related information.
  • Brand profiles: company name, business information, website, industry, contact details and campaign information.
  • Campaign activity: campaign applications, submissions, content links, deliverables, messages, feedback and performance information.
  • Payments: payment, billing, payout and transaction information necessary to process financial activity through the Services.
  • Support: information you provide when contacting Kixo or submitting a support request.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you access the Services, we may automatically collect information such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, approximate location derived from technical information, pages or screens viewed, referring URLs, timestamps, clicks, usage events and diagnostic information.

3.3 Information From Third Parties

Depending on the features you use, we may receive information from service providers, analytics providers, payment processors, authentication providers, social-media platforms, advertising partners, fraud-prevention services, publicly available sources and other business partners.

4. What Types of Personal Information Do We Collect?

Depending on how you use the Services, the categories of information may include:

  • Identifiers: name, username, email, phone number, account ID, IP address and similar identifiers.
  • Account information: login credentials, profile information, account preferences and role information.
  • Professional or commercial information: business details, creator category, campaign history, rates, services, brand information and transaction history.
  • Internet or network activity: device information, browser information, interaction with our websites and applications, and usage logs.
  • Creator content and media: profile images, campaign submissions, links, videos, images, captions and other content you choose to provide.
  • Payment information: billing details, payout details, transaction records and information supplied by payment providers.
  • Communications: messages, support requests, feedback and other communications with Kixo.
  • Analytics and inferences: information derived from usage or campaign activity to operate, measure and improve the Services.
  • Preferences: language, notification, privacy and account settings.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information unless it is reasonably necessary for a specific lawful purpose and permitted by applicable law.

5. How Do We Use the Personal Information We Collect?

We may use information for legitimate business and service purposes, including:

  • Creating, maintaining and securing user accounts.
  • Providing creator, brand, campaign and marketplace functionality.
  • Matching creators and brands with relevant campaigns and opportunities.
  • Processing payments, payouts, refunds and transaction records.
  • Verifying accounts, preventing fraud, abuse, manipulation and unauthorized activity.
  • Communicating about campaigns, account activity, support requests, service updates and security matters.
  • Providing customer support and resolving disputes.
  • Measuring campaign performance and improving products, features and user experience.
  • Conducting analytics, debugging, testing and service monitoring.
  • Sending promotional communications where permitted by law and, where required, based on your consent.
  • Complying with legal obligations, lawful requests and applicable regulatory requirements.
  • Protecting the rights, property, security and integrity of Kixo, its users and others.

5.1 Legal Bases

Where required by applicable law, we may rely on consent, performance of a contract, legitimate interests, compliance with legal obligations, protection of rights and other lawful bases available to us.

6. When and With Whom Do We Share Personal Information?

We may disclose information to the following categories of recipients when reasonably necessary to operate the Services or for other purposes described in this Policy:

  • Service providers: hosting, cloud storage, analytics, communications, customer support, security and technical providers.
  • Payment providers: entities that process payments, payouts, refunds and financial transactions.
  • Brands and creators: information needed to facilitate campaigns, collaborations, applications, deliverables and campaign administration.
  • Marketing and advertising partners: where permitted by applicable law and consistent with your choices.
  • Fraud and security providers: to detect abuse, fraud, malicious activity or violations of our terms.
  • Professional advisers: lawyers, accountants, auditors and other advisers when reasonably necessary.
  • Government or law enforcement: when required or permitted by law, legal process or a valid governmental request.
  • Business transaction parties: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets or similar transaction.

We do not disclose personal information to third parties for purposes outside those described in this Policy unless we have a lawful basis to do so or provide additional notice where required.

7. Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies

Kixo may use cookies, local storage, pixels, SDKs, logs and similar technologies to operate the Services, remember preferences, understand usage, maintain security and measure performance.

Technologies may include:

  • Essential technologies required for authentication, security and core functionality.
  • Preference technologies used to remember settings such as language or display preferences.
  • Analytics technologies used to understand how users interact with the Services.
  • Advertising technologies where used and permitted, which may help measure campaigns or deliver relevant advertising.

You can often control cookies through your browser or device settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect the availability or functionality of parts of the Services.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have some or all of the following rights:

  • Right to know/access: request information about the personal information we maintain about you.
  • Right to correct: request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to delete: request deletion of personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to data portability: request a copy of certain information in a portable format where applicable.
  • Right to withdraw consent: withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Right to opt out of certain marketing: unsubscribe from promotional communications.
  • Right to object or restrict: object to or request restriction of certain processing where provided by applicable law.
  • Right to complain: contact the applicable data protection authority where permitted by law.

These rights are subject to applicable legal limitations, verification requirements and exceptions.

9. How to Exercise Your Privacy Rights

To request access, correction, deletion or another privacy action, contact us using the details in Section 17. Please include enough information for us to understand and verify your request.

We may ask for additional information to verify that you are the person associated with the relevant account or are authorized to make the request. We will process verified requests within the time required by applicable law.

If we deny a request where applicable law gives you a right to appeal, we will provide information about the available appeal process.

You can also delete your account and associated personal data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account in the app, or via our account deletion page.

10. Children's Privacy

The Services are not intended for children who are under the minimum age required to use the Services under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to Kixo in circumstances where collection was not permitted, please contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

11. International Users and Data Transfers

Kixo and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country in which you live. Those countries may have data-protection laws that differ from the laws in your jurisdiction.

Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we may use appropriate mechanisms such as contractual protections or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms.

12. European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, additional rights may apply under applicable data-protection laws, including the GDPR or equivalent local legislation.

12.1 Legal Bases

Depending on the processing activity, our legal basis may include performance of a contract, legitimate interests, consent, compliance with legal obligations or another basis recognized by applicable law.

12.2 Your Rights

  • Access your personal information.
  • Request rectification of inaccurate information.
  • Request erasure in applicable circumstances.
  • Request restriction of processing in applicable circumstances.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing, where applicable.
  • Request portability of information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

12.3 Complaints

You may contact the relevant data protection authority in your country if you believe your rights have been violated.

12.4 International Transfers

Where personal information is transferred outside the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we will use appropriate safeguards where required by applicable law.

13. Promotional Communications

Where permitted by law, Kixo may send service-related and promotional communications by email, SMS, push notification or other communication channels.

You can generally stop promotional email communications by using the unsubscribe mechanism included in the message. You may also contact us at infokixoads@gmail.com.

Even if you opt out of promotional communications, we may continue to send non-promotional messages that are necessary to operate your account, process transactions, provide security notices or administer the Services.

14. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse or destruction.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Accordingly, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to or stored by the Services.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and should notify us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

15. Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain business and transaction records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent fraud and protect our rights.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the purpose for which it was collected, legal requirements and operational needs.

When personal information is no longer required, we may delete it, anonymize it or securely isolate it, subject to applicable legal and technical requirements.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Services, technology, business practices or applicable legal requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this Policy. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.

We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.
