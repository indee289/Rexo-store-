Real creators. Real audiences. Real results. These General Campaign Rules apply to creator participation on Kixo. Campaign-specific instructions may impose additional requirements. If a Campaign rule conflicts with a general rule, the more specific Campaign requirement applies for that Campaign.

Important Information

These General Campaign Rules ("Rules") explain the minimum content, authenticity, engagement and submission standards expected from creators using Kixo.

By applying to, accepting or participating in a Campaign, you agree to follow these Rules, the applicable Campaign Details, the Kixo Creator Terms of Use, and applicable advertising and platform policies.

Creators are responsible for reviewing all Campaign requirements before creating or publishing content.

1. Authenticity and Fair Play

Kixo is designed to reward genuine creator activity and authentic audience engagement. Any attempt to artificially manipulate Campaign performance is prohibited.

Artificially inflating views or engagement is not allowed.Creators must not use fake, purchased, automated or otherwise manipulated activity to increase Campaign performance.

1.1 Prohibited Manipulation

  • Using viewbots, engagement bots, click farms or fake views.
  • Purchasing or arranging fake likes, comments, shares, saves, followers or other engagement.
  • Running advertisements solely to artificially inflate a Campaign metric where the Campaign does not expressly permit paid amplification.
  • Using engagement-exchange groups or coordinated artificial engagement schemes.
  • Repeatedly interacting with your own content through additional accounts to inflate metrics.
  • Using unrelated third-party accounts to generate artificial views or engagement.
  • Misrepresenting screenshots, analytics, audience statistics or Campaign results.

Where Kixo or a Brand identifies suspicious activity, a submission may be reviewed before approval or payment.

2. General Content Guidelines

2.1 Relevance

All Campaign submissions must follow the Campaign brief, theme, niche and requested deliverables. Content should be relevant to the creator's audience and the Campaign objective.

2.2 Post Availability

Unless a Campaign specifies a different period, Campaign posts should remain publicly available for at least 30 days after the Campaign end date. Removing, hiding or materially changing a required post earlier may result in disqualification or other Campaign action.

2.3 Public Visibility

Where a Campaign requires public performance metrics, posts and relevant statistics must remain publicly viewable or otherwise verifiable through the applicable platform. Hidden or inaccessible metrics may make a submission ineligible for verification.

2.4 Niche and Audience

Creators should submit content through accounts that are genuinely relevant to the Campaign's required niche. Using an unrelated account simply to qualify for a Campaign may result in rejection.

2.5 Captions and Hashtags

Captions, hashtags, mentions and keywords must be relevant to the submitted content and Campaign. Spammy, misleading or unrelated hashtags may cause rejection.

3. Engagement and Metrics

Campaign-specific engagement requirements will be shown in the Campaign Details. Creators must meet those requirements through genuine audience activity.

  • Likes, comments, shares, saves and views must be authentic.
  • Comments generated by bots, purchased services or unrelated engagement groups do not qualify as genuine engagement.
  • Where a minimum engagement rate is specified, Kixo may verify the rate using available platform data or information submitted by the creator.
  • Creators must not delete qualifying comments or otherwise manipulate visible engagement for the purpose of passing verification.
  • If a Campaign uses a specific metric, that Campaign's definition and verification method will control.
Example: If a Campaign requires a minimum engagement rate, reaching the required number through purchased engagement or coordinated engagement exchanges does not satisfy the requirement.

4. Quality and Originality

4.1 Quality

Submissions should be clear, understandable and reasonably aligned with the quality requirements in the Campaign brief. Low-effort, spammy, misleading or substantially incomplete submissions may be rejected.

4.2 Originality

Creators must submit original work or content they have the legal right to use. Duplicate submissions, copied material and content taken from other creators without permission may be rejected.

4.3 Copyright and Third-Party Rights

Do not use music, footage, photographs, logos, trademarks, clips, fonts or other protected material unless you have the necessary rights or the Campaign expressly provides or permits the required asset.

4.4 No Misleading Claims

Creators must not make false, deceptive or unsupported claims about a product, service, Brand, offer or Campaign.

5. Advertising and Sponsorship Disclosure

Where content is sponsored, paid, incentivized or otherwise subject to disclosure requirements, creators must use clear and conspicuous disclosures required by applicable law and the relevant social platform.

  • Do not hide a required sponsorship disclosure among unrelated hashtags or text.
  • Do not imply that an independently purchased product is a personal recommendation if the Campaign relationship makes that representation misleading.
  • Follow any disclosure wording or placement requirements stated in the Campaign Details.
  • Creators remain responsible for compliance with the advertising rules applicable to their location and audience.

6. Prohibited Content

Unless a Campaign expressly permits otherwise and applicable law allows it, Campaign submissions must not contain:

  • Illegal content or instructions for unlawful activity.
  • Content that infringes copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity or other rights.
  • Malware, malicious files, harmful links or fraudulent schemes.
  • Deceptive, fabricated or materially misleading claims.
  • Content intended to manipulate Campaign verification or platform metrics.
  • Harassment, threats or targeted abuse.
  • Sexually explicit content where prohibited by the Campaign or applicable platform rules.
  • Extremely graphic or gratuitously violent material where prohibited by the Campaign.
  • Content promoting discrimination or hateful attacks against protected groups.
  • Content that violates the rules of the social platform on which it is published.

Additional restrictions may apply to specific Campaigns, industries, products, audiences or jurisdictions.

7. Campaign-Specific Rules

Every Campaign may contain additional instructions covering deliverables, formats, captions, hashtags, mentions, posting windows, minimum duration, audience, engagement, visual requirements, disclosure language, music, logos and other requirements.

7.1 Campaign Brief Controls

Creators must read the full Campaign brief before accepting or submitting work. A submission that satisfies these general Rules but fails a material Campaign-specific requirement may still be rejected.

7.2 No Unauthorized Changes

Creators must not materially alter mandatory assets, logos, templates, product claims, campaign messaging or required placements unless the Campaign expressly permits the change.

7.3 Deadlines

Creators must meet the Campaign's application, creation, approval and publishing deadlines. If a creator anticipates a genuine issue affecting a deadline, they should contact Kixo support as soon as reasonably possible.

8. Music Campaign Guidelines

Music Campaigns may include additional requirements because audio rights and platform policies can vary.

  • Use only the audio, music or sound sources permitted by the Campaign.
  • Do not use sexually explicit, hateful, discriminatory or otherwise prohibited material when the Campaign rules exclude it.
  • Do not combine another Campaign's branding, logo or promotional requirement with the music Campaign unless expressly permitted.
  • Meet the minimum video duration stated in the Campaign Details.
  • Do not materially edit or distort required Campaign audio where the Campaign requires a specified audio asset.
  • Creators remain responsible for complying with the applicable social platform's music and copyright rules.
Campaign-specific audio rules control.If a particular Music Campaign states a different audio duration, format or permitted-use requirement, follow that Campaign's requirement.

9. Logo Campaign Guidelines

9.1 Provided Templates

If Kixo or a Brand provides a required template, creators must use the specified template and dimensions. Unless the Campaign says otherwise, do not move, resize, recolor, distort or replace a mandatory logo asset.

9.2 Logo Visibility

Required logos must remain clearly visible and must not be substantially covered by captions, usernames, buttons, stickers or other platform UI.

9.3 Animated Logos

Where an animated logo is provided, the Campaign may require the logo to remain visible for the specified duration and to loop or animate without interruption.

9.4 Placement

Creators must follow the Campaign's approved logo placement and any defined safe zones. Placement that materially obstructs the required logo or places it outside the approved area may result in rejection.

9.5 Asset Integrity

Do not apply filters, effects, stretching, recoloring or other modifications to a mandatory logo asset unless expressly allowed by the Campaign.

10. Submission and Review

10.1 Submission Accuracy

Information submitted for verification must be truthful and complete. Screenshots, URLs, statistics and other evidence must correspond to the actual Campaign submission.

10.2 Review

Kixo or the Brand may review submissions for compliance with the Campaign Details and these Rules. Review may include content quality, authenticity, public availability, required disclosures, metrics and other Campaign criteria.

10.3 Revisions

If revisions are permitted, creators must make requested changes within the stated timeframe. A revision request does not guarantee final approval or payment.

10.4 Approval

Campaign approval means the submission has passed the applicable review process. Approval does not waive responsibility for later-discovered violations or inaccurate information.

11. Violations and Enforcement

Failure to follow these Rules or Campaign-specific requirements may result in one or more of the following actions, depending on the nature and severity of the issue:

  • Submission rejection.
  • Request for correction or resubmission.
  • Campaign disqualification.
  • Loss of eligibility for a specific Campaign.
  • Restriction or suspension of account features.
  • Suspension or termination of the creator account.
  • Review or withholding of disputed or ineligible Campaign payments where permitted by applicable law and the applicable agreement.
  • Reporting activity to a relevant platform, Brand or authority where legally appropriate.
Repeated or serious manipulation may result in a permanent account restriction.This includes artificial engagement, fraudulent submissions, impersonation, repeated Campaign abuse or deliberate attempts to bypass Kixo's verification systems.

11.1 Appeals and Support

If you believe a Campaign decision or enforcement action was made in error, contact Kixo support with the relevant Campaign ID, submission details and supporting information. Kixo may review the matter according to its internal procedures and applicable law.
