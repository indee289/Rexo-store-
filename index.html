Our Story

Built in Jodhpur.
Scaling Globally.

Rexo Agency was founded in Jodhpur, Rajasthan, with a singular mission: make influencer marketing accessible, accountable, and scalable for brands of every size. We saw brands wasting budgets on unverified creators and campaigns with no measurement — and we built the solution.

We focus on two high-growth markets: India — one of the world's largest and fastest-moving creator economies — and Latin America (LATAM), where influencer marketing is experiencing explosive growth with significantly lower competition. Our bilingual team navigates both markets with deep cultural fluency.

At the heart of our operation is Campster, our proprietary influencer marketplace app. Campster connects brands with verified creators, manages campaign workflows, tracks submissions, and handles payments — all in one platform. It's the technology layer that makes Rexo campaigns faster, more transparent, and more effective than traditional agencies.
